Interpretation:
Request state remains in its KV-pool slot when packed rows change; graph padding has disjoint slots. Prefill still drains pending decode and restores admitted or retracted requests. No state-update fusion is enabled in this comparison.
All per-pass results are disclosed. The shared host, stochastic sampling and dynamic batching limit interpretation of small throughput differences; two passes are descriptive evidence rather than a formal performance confidence interval.
No new PR is opened by this archive. The implementation and evidence remain on the experimental branch.

Validation: 232 CPU and 3 GPU regressions; 1866 state-row comparisons, 384 delayed snapshot resolves, 366 row changes, 12 slot reuses, 6 retraction restores and 12 EOS checks; 12/12 real-model code arrays match in each of dynamic-bucket and fixed-batch-8 controls.
