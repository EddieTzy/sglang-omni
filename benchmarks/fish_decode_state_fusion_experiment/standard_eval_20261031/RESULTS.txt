Interpretation:
The isolated state update reduces 16 GPU operations to one kernel. Serving and quality findings above use the complete previous-PR workload and supersede the earlier fixed-128-token exploratory comparison for this workload.
All per-pass results are disclosed. The shared host, stochastic sampling and dynamic batching limit interpretation of small throughput differences; two passes are descriptive evidence rather than a formal performance confidence interval.
